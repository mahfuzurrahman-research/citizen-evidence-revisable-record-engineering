# Scientific and Claim Boundary

All inputs and outcomes are synthetic engineering fixtures. No empirical field data, private paper result, exact private formal design or recovered scientific history appears in this companion.

The score is a hand-declared weighted index. Its threshold routes review; it is not a probability of truth or wrongdoing. A low score or deferred route does not establish that a record is correct. Missing evidence signals remain missing.

Verification records one of `SUPPORTED`, `CONTRADICTED` or `INCONCLUSIVE` against an exact evidence revision, hash and expiry. Distinct actor IDs check a toy separation rule; they do not authenticate people or prove source independence. Hashes detect consistency changes; they do not establish truth.

The record claim allowlist is intentionally narrow:

| Allowed target | Supported statement | Unsupported escalation |
|---|---|---|
| `record_present` | This evidence revision is currently present | Lawful authority or evidentiary truth |
| `verification_recorded` | A current verification outcome is recorded | Inconclusive evidence becomes supported truth |
| `correction_recorded` | A current correction event cites supported verification | Successful remedy, lawful authority or causal effect |

Unknown targets are blocked. Current validity is revisable: new evidence supersedes support, withdrawal retracts it, replacement changes the active witnesses, and verification expires at the declared boundary.

Passing tests and SQL reconciliation establish software consistency with the public toy specification. They do not establish scientific validity, causal effects, empirical findings, deployment effectiveness, population fairness or successful administrative remedy. Descriptive precision/recall on fabricated labels must not be presented as field performance.
